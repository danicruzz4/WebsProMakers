The café

A brick coffee house on the beach

Ngopi Pantai sits on the sand at Cenang Hill. Inside: exposed brick, lots of plants and a breeze through the room. Outside: tables facing the sea and one of the best sunsets in Pantai Tengah. Come in the morning for breakfast or after 6 pm for the sunset: the afternoon sun is strong.

How to get here

  1. Follow Google Maps to Cenang Hill Beachfront (the link below).
  2. At the fork next to the construction site, take the left road.
  3. The road fits one car at a time: keep going until the small car park by the beach.
