Our story

A family table, far from home

The Argan Trees is run by a family from Luxembourg. The room looks like Morocco, the plates are European: fresh bruschetta, pasta and ravioli, slow-cooked beef bourguignon and steaks. Guests often say it is their favourite restaurant on the island, and a good break from local food after a few days.
