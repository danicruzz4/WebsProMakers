The restaurant

A taste of Damascus in Pantai Tengah

Syrian and Arabic cooking served generously: cold and hot mezze, rice platters from across the Arab world, grills and home-style stews. Eat in a quiet dining room with private booths and lanterns, or outside on the terrace.
