Ask a question
Why does my business need a website?

A website gives your business a permanent, organised place online. Customers can check your menu, services, location and contact details at any time, and it complements your social media and your Google profile.

Can you create a website for my restaurant?

Yes. Restaurant websites are one of our specialities: menu with photos and prices, opening hours, location, WhatsApp and reservation requests. Have a look at the restaurant demo above.

Can my website have multiple languages?

Yes. We can build your website in several languages, such as English, Bahasa Melayu, Chinese or others your customers speak.

Can customers contact me through WhatsApp?

Yes. We can add WhatsApp buttons that open a chat with your business, with a pre-written message.

Can you include my menu and prices?

Yes. We can include your full menu or catalogue with categories, photos and prices.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every website we build is designed for phones first and also works on tablets and computers.

How long does it take to create a website?

It depends on the size of the website and the features you need. We give you an estimated timeline together with your personalised proposal.

Can I request changes to my website?

Yes. You can request changes during the design process. Changes after launch are agreed according to each project.

Do you offer website maintenance?

Maintenance and updates can be offered depending on the characteristics of each project. We will talk through the options with you.

Can you help my business appear on Google?

We build your website following good SEO practices and can help you link it to your Google Business Profile. No one can guarantee specific positions in search results.

Can you create a QR code for my restaurant menu?

Yes. We can create a digital menu and a QR code that opens it, ready to print for your tables.

How much does a website cost?

Every business is different. Our websites are tailored to each client's needs, design preferences and required features. Contact us to receive a personalised proposal.
